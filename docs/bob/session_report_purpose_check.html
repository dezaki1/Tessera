Tessera — Purpose Check Robustness

Task report · Art. 54 prohibited-purpose hardening · 30 Oct 2026

Objective

Make the Art. 54 prohibited-purpose classifier in ehds/decision.py robust to new and indirect wording, without loosening it so far that legitimate research requests are rejected. The worst failure mode (approving a prohibited purpose) was to be eliminated; the second worst (rejecting a legitimate request) was equally penalised.

Step 1 — Adversarial test set

Created tests/test_purpose_robustness.py with 100 parametrised cases before touching any rule code:

  • 43 prohibited texts — varied wording, indirect phrasing, some non-native English, at least 5 per Art. 54 category, including the hard cases named in the brief.
  • 57 allowed texts — legitimate purposes that resemble prohibited ones (insurance cost studies, smoking-cessation research, marketing authorisation, occupational health, study-exclusion criteria, etc.).

Baseline run: 16 failures — all in the prohibited set (missed cases); no false positives.

Step 3 — Rule changes in ehds/decision.py

Insurance / credit rule

  • Extended forward match window from 80 → 150 chars.
  • Added underwriting, actuarial, policyholders, claimants as standalone signals near pricing vocabulary.
  • Added standalone premium tiers/levels/adjust and policy pricing patterns.
  • Added bidirectional price … insurance pattern (catches "price our life-insurance products").
  • Extended loan pattern: loan (approval|conditions|decisions|terms|rates|eligibility).
  • Added price accordingly pattern.
  • Added exclude … from (our/their) insurance/coverage/benefits/products pattern.

Employment decisions rule

  • Added job candidates? and candidates? to shortlist.
  • Added noun form terminat\w+ (?:of )?(?:their|the)? employment.
  • Added reverse-order pattern: workers/employees/staff → dismiss/retain.
  • Added redundancy near select/decision/list/shortlist.

Harmful products rule

  • Added apps?, platform\w*, engaging, design\w* to the commercial-context list (catches "design a more engaging betting app").
  • Extended exceptions: tobacco control, control polic\w+, epidemiolog\w+, prevalence, public health, inform\w* (policy|prevention|guidelines).

Decisions against individuals rule

  • deny/refuse/blacklist near person group — removed requirement for explicit health reason (the action against the group is itself the signal).
  • Added exclude … from our/their coverage/services/products/programme/benefits.
  • Added decide … to exclude/blacklist/deny pattern.
  • Added exceptions for study-exclusion phrasing: from the analysis/study/cohort/sample/dataset/model and excluded due to missing/age/criteria.

Known blind spots (remaining)

  • Purely financial language without insurance/credit vocabulary ("fiscal risk of our portfolio") may slip past the insurance rule.
  • Novel substance brand names not in the trigger list are not caught.
  • Very indirect employment decisions ("help HR understand attendance patterns") are only flagged for staff review, not rejected.
  • Art. 54(5) — activities conflicting with national ethical provisions — is not checked by rule.
